# timing

Pre-market (7:00 AM – 9:30 AM ET) → 8:00 PM – 10:30 PM China time, same calendar day
Regular hours (9:30 AM – 4:00 PM ET) → 10:30 PM (same day) – 5:00 AM (next day) China time
After-hours (4:00 PM – 8:00 PM ET) → 5:00 AM – 9:00 AM China time, next day

# bullish strategy

- bullish - buy - easiest, no expiry
- super bullish - sell puts - can go below strike price or at the strike price and still make money
- max bullish - buy calls - need price to go up fast

# options lesson

- when market expensive, consider selling call options
- "align ourselves with ceos" - ceos have stock options
- short duration options - gambling
- on dips and cheap markets - sell put options, buy call options, buy shares
- example sell 2 year put contract in bear market
  - sell a put = buy stock at a strike price - get paid up front for the promise
